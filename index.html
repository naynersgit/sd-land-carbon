Reading this data

  • Source: CARB 2025 Natural and Working Lands Carbon Inventory, aggregated data table. Carbon is metric tons of carbon (MT C), not CO2e. Multiply by 3.664 for CO2e.
  • The data is aggregated by year. It shows how much of each land cover there is, not which acres converted to what, so it can't support a transition (Sankey) view.
  • The 2011 shrubland/grassland reversal and the 2014 shifts in cropland and developed land happen in a single year. Treat them as breaks in the series, not real change.
  • Ownership filters let you see where change happened. The ownership acreage itself stays nearly constant across years.
Data: California Air Resources Board, Natural and Working Lands Inventory.
